title: "Data sources"
description: "Where the tracker's phones, repos and states come from, how each row says so, and why Reddit posts stay claims."
---

Every state on the tracker says where it came from: its `detail` names the source and its
`source` links it. The UI shows that link next to the state.

## Where rows come from

| Source | How a row says so |
|---|---|
| A repo's maintainer, through the site, the CLI or their coding agent | their own detail and source: a commit, a log, a capture |
| OneiOS's own docs, for the S24 Ultra and the phones its fork work was merged or ported from | detail ending `(OneiOS maintainer, date)` |
| r/hackios posts and their porters' comments | detail starting `reddit claim (YYYY-MM-DD):`, source the thread |
| The [HakiOS ports registry](https://hakios-developers.com) | detail starting `HakiOS registry:`, source the port's registry page |
| [hackiosx.dev](https://hackiosx.dev/projects) | detail starting `hackiosx.dev`, source the section of its projects page |
| GitHub (repo metadata, fork parents and READMEs) | the repo's lineage and family on the Repos tab |

A row a maintainer wrote is never overwritten by a synced source: the registry, hackiosx.dev and
Reddit only fill rows that are empty, unknown or an older claim of their own, and never lower a
state. Phone photos are the makers' official product images or freely licensed Wikimedia Commons
files, credited under each photo.

## Why Reddit posts stay claims

A Reddit post is the porter's own statement, usually a video or a screenshot. Nobody has rebuilt
or rerun it. So:

- A status taken from a thread has `detail` starting with `reddit claim (YYYY-MM-DD):`, the date
  the thread was read, and `source` set to the thread's permalink.
- A fork found through Reddit is `fork-claim` until someone reproduces it on hardware.
- Nothing is inferred beyond what the post says. An area the post does not mention stays
  `unknown`.

The Updates tab lists the port threads, with a thumbnail from each post.

To replace a claim with a verified state, a maintainer of the phone writes the status with their
own detail and source (a log, a commit, a session folder). See [Maintain a repo](/docs/maintain-a-repo)
and [Agent system](/docs/agents).
